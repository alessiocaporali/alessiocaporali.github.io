01 — About

About me

Short bio

Junior Assistant Professor (fixed-term) at the University of Bologna since 2024, working at the intersection of robotics and machine learning. In April 2024, I successfully defended my PhD thesis titled “Robotic Perception and Manipulation of Deformable Linear Objects”, advised by Prof. Gianluca Palli.

Research interests

My research focuses on the perception and manipulation of deformable objects, aiming to advance the capabilities of robotic systems in handling complex objects. My primary research interests include:

  1. 01
    Computer vision and tactile sensing

    Developing algorithms to enhance a robot’s understanding of target objects and their environment.

  2. 02
    Robotic manipulation

    Particularly addressing tasks such as grasping, sliding, and motion planning.

  3. 03
    Deformable object modeling

    To predict and control their behavior.

  4. 04
    Deep learning for physical systems

    Leveraging neural network approximations of dynamical systems to facilitate robotic tasks.
